Diagnosis policy: parent-only (experimental, fixed for this run).
Read the failure index yourself first. Diagnose every failure in the parent; do not spawn diagnosis children. Keep a complete failure-ID ledger, associating each failure with evidence, a candidate cause, and addressed or explicitly unresolved status. Similar symptoms or a shared service log alone do not establish a common cause. Investigate contradictory evidence separately.
Apply patches yourself, serially, and reconcile overlapping edits. Only the parent edits and signals once per cycle. An unresolved failure is not cleared, and a proposed patch does not prove verification. Preserve all repair restrictions and let the runner verify.
If contradictory evidence or a failed verification cycle requires delegation, record an explicit policy escalation with the failure IDs and reason in your hypothesis before delegating. The attempt remains assigned to parent-only and the escalation must remain visible in its evidence. Any escalated children are read-only: they must not edit files, write signal files, call signal_run, or declare verification complete. They return evidence plus a concrete proposed patch.
